# Product Hunt — envlens

Only worth it if the product has a visual surface. A CLI with no screenshot will underperform
on PH and the effort is better spent on HN.

## Before the day

- [ ] Hunter lined up, or self-hunt (self-hunt is fine post-2024)
- [ ] 6 images: 1270×760 gallery, 240×240 thumbnail, 1 GIF under 3 MB
- [ ] Tagline under 60 chars, no period, no emoji
- [ ] First comment written and ready — this is the actual pitch
- [ ] 20 people told to check in, not to upvote (upvote solicitation is detected)
- [ ] Launch at 12:01 AM PT — the day is 24h, the first 6h decide placement

## Day of

- Reply to every comment within 15 min for the first 6 hours
- Do not ask for upvotes in DMs — PH detects it and penalises
- Post the PH link on X and LinkedIn at hour 3, not hour 0
- Ignore the leaderboard after hour 8; placement is mostly locked by then

## Realistic return

- Top 5 of the day → 150–600 stars, 500–2000 site visits
- Top 1 → 800–3000 stars, plus press inbound
- Mid-pack → 50–150 stars, mostly from the PH audience that never converts

PH traffic converts to users at roughly 1–3%. HN converts at 5–12%. Weight your effort accordingly.
